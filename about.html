About this book

MCA Year 2 Notes is a free, chapter-wise study companion for the MCA Second Year (Lateral Entry) programme of M.P. Bhoj (Open) University, Bhopal. It follows the official MCA Second Year syllabus: four theory subjects, two practical labs and a project.

The pages are arranged like a book — a table of index, one page per unit, and Previous / Next navigation — so you can study in order or jump straight to a topic.

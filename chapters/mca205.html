MCA 205 (P)

🧪 DBMS Lab

Max / Min marks: 100 / 50Practical

SQL practicals on the Salesmen, Customers and Orders tables (45 queries).

Tables used: Salesmen (SNUM, SNAME, CITY, COMMISSION), Customers (CNUM, CNAME, CITY, RATING, SNUM) and Orders (ONUM, AMOUNT, ODATE, CNUM, SNUM).

Suggested practical list — 45 SQL queries

  1. Produce the order no, amount and date of all orders.
  2. All information about all customers with salesman number 1001.
  3. Display city, sname, snum and commission (in that order).
  4. Rating followed by the name of each customer in Surat.
  5. snum of all salesmen with orders, without duplicates.
  6. All orders for more than Rs. 1000.
  7. Names and cities of salesmen in London with commission above 10%.
  8. Customers whose names begin with 'C'.
  9. Customers whose names begin with a letter from 'A' to 'G'.
  10. Orders with zero or NULL amount.
  11. Largest orders of salesman 1002 and 1007.
  12. Count all orders of October 3, 1997.
  13. Total amount ordered.
  14. Average amount ordered.
  15. Count the salesmen currently having orders.
  16. All salesmen with their % of commission.
  17. Assuming 12% commission: order number, salesman no and commission for each order.
  18. Highest rating in each city, as: For the city (city), the highest rating is: (rating).
  19. All customers in descending order of rating.
  20. Total of orders for each day, in descending order.
  21. Names of all customers with their salesman's name.
  22. Customers and salesmen who share the same city.
  23. All orders with the names of their customer and salesman.
  24. Orders by customers not located in the same city as their salesman.
  25. Customers serviced by salespeople with commission above 12%.
  26. Salesman commission on each order by a customer with rating above 100.
  27. All pairs of customers having the same rating, without duplication.
  28. Orders greater than the average of October 4, 1997.
  29. Average commission of salesmen in London.
  30. Orders attributed to salesmen in 'London' using both subquery and join.
  31. Commission of all salesmen serving customers in 'London'.
  32. Customers whose cnum is 1000 above the snum of Sejal.
  33. Count customers with rating above the average of 'Surat'.
  34. Salesmen with customers located in their cities using ANY and IN.
  35. Salesmen for whom there are customers that follow them in alphabetical order.
  36. Customers with rating greater than any customer in 'Rome'.
  37. Orders with amount greater than at least one of the orders from 6th October, 1997.
  38. Orders with amounts smaller than any amount for a customer in 'London'.
  39. Customers with a greater rating than every customer in 'Rome'.
  40. UNION of two queries: names, cities, ratings of all customers; rating >= 200 shows 'HIGH RATING', otherwise 'LOW RATING'.
  41. Name and number of each salesman and customer with more than one current order, alphabetical by name.
  42. UNION of three queries: salesmen in Surat (snum), customers in Surat (cnum), orders of 3rd Oct (onum) — duplicates retained between the last two only.
  43. Remove all orders from customer Chirag.
  44. Set the ratings of all customers of Piyush to 400.
  45. Increase the rating of all customers in Rome by 100.
